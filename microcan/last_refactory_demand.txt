  Conclusion
  Yes: use C++23 abstractions for protocol parsing/serialization layers first; keep HAL/ISR byte-pump paths low-level where needed. That gives clearer
  intent and fewer signedness/endian bugs without forcing “high-level everywhere”.

  - Review is static (no runtime benchmark here).
